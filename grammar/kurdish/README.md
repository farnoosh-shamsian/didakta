# Didakta Grammar for Annotation — Kurdish

The Central Kurdish (Sorani) edition of Didakta.

- **Grammar:** Farnoosh Shamsian (Leipzig University), after H. W. Smyth's *Greek Grammar for Colleges* (1920).
- **Translation:** Farshid Rahimi, Allameh Tabataba'i University, Tehran.
- **Version of record:** the Zenodo deposit, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11221442

Cite the translation as:

> Rahimi, F., Shamsian, F., & Smyth, H. W. (2024). *Didakta Grammar for Annotation (Kurdish)* [Dataset].
> Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11221442
