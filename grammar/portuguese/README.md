# Didakta Grammar for Annotation — Brazilian Portuguese

The Brazilian Portuguese edition of Didakta (*Didakta Gramática para Anotação*).

- **Grammar:** Farnoosh Shamsian (Leipzig University), after H. W. Smyth's *Greek Grammar for Colleges* (1920).
- **Translation and revision:** Anise D'Orange Ferreira. The translation was made with the help of DeepL
  and then corrected and revised by hand against the Greek and the English.
- **Original repository:** [ProjetosAbertosClassicasDigitais/Didakta-br-portuguese](https://github.com/ProjetosAbertosClassicasDigitais/Didakta-br-portuguese/),
  where Anise D'Orange Ferreira published the translation in July 2024 (docx, PDF and EPUB).
- **Translator's notes:** [`translator-notes.md`](translator-notes.md): Anise D'Orange Ferreira's notes on how the
  machine translation was revised (pronouns, the imperfect, the present perfect), copied from that
  repository's README.

Cite the translation as:

> Ferreira, A. D'O. (2024). *Didakta Grammar for Annotation — Versão em Português*.
> https://github.com/ProjetosAbertosClassicasDigitais/Didakta-br-portuguese/

## Source and corrections

The files are built from the translator's Google Doc as it stood on 17 November 2026. Its text is
identical to the docx, PDF and EPUB in the original repository: the July 2024 upload and the
Google Doc differ only in page margins.

Before the files were built, the reviewer comments on the Google Doc were resolved, clear errors
(mostly left over from the machine translation) were corrected, and the formatting was made consistent.
Wording that is merely debatable was left as the translator wrote it. The details are in the
[main README](../../README.md#brazilian-portuguese-edition).
