# Commentary terms and Didakta tags

19th-century commentaries (Holden, Gorham, Bigg, Goodwin & White, Breitenbach, Hertlein–Nitsche, Rehdantz–Carnuth, Krüger) name constructions in their own words, in English, German or Latin. This table maps those words to Didakta tags.

How to read it:
- When a note uses a term that maps to **one** tag, that is strong evidence for that tag.
- When the term maps to **two** tags, the note narrows the choice to those two. Decide between them with the Didakta cards (`annotation/reference/didakta-tag-cards.md`).
- A term not listed here is no evidence until you have checked what the commentator means by it.
